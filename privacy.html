Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

My Little Universe is a private memory album intended for two authorized users.

Information We Process

Google Sign-In provides account information such as email address, name and profile picture to identify authorized users.

Google Drive Access

The application is being developed to use Google Drive for storing private memories. Google Drive access will require separate authorization before it becomes operational.

Access Control

The application is intended to restrict access to approved accounts. Administrative actions are intended to be limited to the designated administrator.

Data Sharing

Personal memories are not intended for public sharing or advertising. Google authentication and hosting providers process technical information needed to operate the service.

Data Retention and Deletion

Stored memories will remain until removed by the authorized administrator, subject to the storage provider's retention policies. Permanent storage is not yet enabled.

Security

We use Google account authentication and server-side identity checks. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the contact information provided during authorization.
